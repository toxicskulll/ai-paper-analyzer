****CHALLENGES IN COMPUTER VISION AND REINFORCEMENT LEARNING**

This section elucidates several challenges associated with the existing works on computer vision and reinforcement learning, emphasizing medical image segmentation and energy efficiency in data centers.

1. **Medical Image Segmentation**
   The primary challenge lies in the scarcity of datasets for training and validating models, which may lead to suboptimal performance [13]. To mitigate this, it is crucial to validate the performance of these models on a diverse range of datasets to ensure their efficacy in real-life scenarios. Additionally, efforts should be directed towards optimizing the models specifically for healthcare applications to improve accuracy and efficiency.

2. **Energy Efficiency in Data Centers**
   The optimization and validation of AI techniques, such as Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL), in practical settings is essential to confirm their effectiveness in large-scale data centers [18]. Furthermore, the DRL approach must be scaled up for larger data centers while preserving its efficiency and sustainability benefits. Lastly, there is a need to explore the applicability of these optimized AI techniques beyond data centers, including cloud providers and scientific research institutions.

Addressing these challenges will enable future studies to enhance the performance of medical image segmentation models and reinforcement learning approaches for energy-efficient data center management, thereby contributing substantially to both healthcare and sustainability.
