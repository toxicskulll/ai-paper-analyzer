**Abstract**

This paper presents a comprehensive literature review, delving into recent advancements in the fields of computer vision and reinforcement learning with a primary focus on medical image segmentation and energy efficiency in data center management. The literature is systematically categorized based on their objectives, methodologies, results, key contributions, limitations, and potential future directions.

In the context of medical image segmentation, this review explores two notable studies: 3D U-Net and MedNeXt. The 3D U-Net employs an adapted U-net architecture to address challenges in 3D medical image segmentation, demonstrating improved accuracy compared to previous approaches on benchmark datasets. Meanwhile, MedNeXt leverages a transformer-based network architecture for accurate medical image segmentation tasks, exhibiting superior performance over existing methods. However, both studies emphasize the need for larger datasets and real-life validation of their models' performance. Future research in this area includes optimizing these AI techniques for specific healthcare applications to achieve better performance.

On the other hand, the review also examines energy efficiency strategies within data centers through reinforcement learning. The study on Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) for adaptive cooling control strategies demonstrates up to 10% energy savings compared to conventional methods, highlighting a novel approach that could improve sustainability and efficiency in data center management. Nevertheless, the need for real-world validation and optimization of AI techniques for specific applications in data centers is suggested as future work. This research paves the way for scaling up the DRL approach for large-scale data centers and exploring its applicability across various industries, such as cloud providers and scientific research institutions. The unified literature review presented in this paper offers valuable insights into the state-of-the-art advancements in computer vision and reinforcement learning and suggests promising future research directions in medical image segmentation and energy efficiency in data centers.
